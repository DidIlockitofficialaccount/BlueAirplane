At BlueAirplane privacy, is our top priority. 
So here's how we handle your data :
1. Servers
Your data is not accessed by us. We do not store your data nor do we use it.
Your data stays in CloudKit and every pin gets reset after 30 days. This has multible benefits :
A) Moderation
B) Storage saving
C) Keeping the map clean for anyone.
You have the right to be forgotten. To edit or delete your pins, please contact us. (More about that below)
And all the data that is in CloudKit is needed for the app to function. Such as : PinLocation (aka where it is)
and messageContents. To put it simply : We only have access to messages and their location.
None of this is linked to you.
2. Advertising
Advertising is not yet present in our app.
So this section is for the future of BlueAirplane.
If and when we impliment Advertising you can choose if you want personalised ads or not.
This won't affect the functionality of the app.
